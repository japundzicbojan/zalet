# Zalet  -  60-second pitch

## Hook (10s)

Founders can ship product. Most can’t ship a week of ads about that product.
Zalet turns a product URL into a filmable UGC campaign in about a minute.

## What it does (20s)

1. **Firecrawl** reads your site.
2. **Exa** finds what’s working in your category right now.
3. **Grok (xAI)** writes a 7-day plan + scripts (including Serbian).
4. **Fal** generates vertical UGC stills.
5. **Daytona** packs everything into a zip you can download.
6. Shareable live board on **Render** (Convex optional for durability).

## Who it’s for (10s)

Solo founders advertising **their own** product. Not an agency tool. Not brand fluff.
You film it. You post it. Zalet just removes the blank page.

## Live demo (15s)

Paste URL → board opens immediately → watch partner trace fill → open scripts → download zip → share `/c/[id]`.

## Ask (5s)

We’re using the official partner stack end-to-end. Happy to show a fresh run live.
